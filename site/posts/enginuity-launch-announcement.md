I'm excited to announce the launch of [Enginuity](https://enginuity.epeters.io) &mdash; a unified interface for all of my analysis tools! 

### Convenient Engineering Tools, Right in Your Browser

The initial release includes updated versions of my existing apps plus two new modules:

- <b>Galvanic Compatibility</b> &ndash; Quickly visualize required surface treatments to prevent galvanic corrosion between pairs of dissimilar metals per MIL-STD-889C.
- <b>Stressed</b> &ndash; Instantly analyze complex stress states. Plot Mohr's Circle and calculate principal and equivalent stresses for any 2D or 3D stress tensor.
- <b>Unit Converter</b> &ndash; Convert those obscure engineering units (slinch, anyone?) without digging through reference tables.
- <b>Random Number Generator</b> &ndash; Generate repeatable sequences of pseudo-random numbers based on a seed phrase.

### Privacy First
Your data stays yours, period. Enginuity is built as a static site, which means:

-   **100% Private:** No accounts. No uploads.
-   **Locals Only**: All calculations are performed on your device. No data is ever sent to an external server.
-   **Works Offline:** No internet connection required once loaded. Install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and use it anywhere. <i>(coming soon!)</i>
